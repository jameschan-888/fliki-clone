# Fliki Pricing

> Source: https://fliki.ai/pricing  Fetched: 2026-07-23

## Tier 1: Free
- $0, no credit card required
- 3 credits per month (detail page says 36)
- 300 voices across 80+ languages
- Create videos from ideas, scripts, blog posts, or PPTs
- Stock images, video clips & music assets
- Generate AI images & thumbnails
- HD 720p
- Includes Fliki watermark

## Tier 2: Standard
- $21 per user / month (annual, was $28)
- 2160 credits per year
- 1000 voices with 500 ultra-realistic
- Full HD 1080p
- Videos up to 15 minutes, translated to 80+ languages
- Voice cloning + limited stock avatars
- AI Playground & commercial rights

## Tier 3: Premium
- $66 per user / month (annual, was $88)
- 7200 credits per year
- 2000+ voices, 1000+ ultra-realistic & multilingual expressive
- Videos up to 40 minutes
- Generate AI video clips + photo avatars
- All AI avatars + multiple voice cloning
- Multiple brand kits, custom fonts & priority support

## Tier 4: Enterprise
- Custom pricing, billed yearly
- Custom credits, bulk discounts & invoiced billing
- API access & state-of-the-art AI models
- Personalized avatars & professional voice cloning
- Branded custom templates & team collaboration
- Dedicated account manager

## Key notes
- 6 core workflows across all plans (varying limits): Idea to video, Script to video, Blog post to video, PPT to video, Auto edit video, Recording to polished video
- Plus 6 audio / thumbnail workflows: Idea to audio, Script to audio, Blog to audio, Idea to thumbnail, Idea to social carousel, Idea to presentation
- Credits deducted based on duration of audio generated or video exported
- Standard plan: 180 min credits -> ~60 short 1-min videos, or 12x5-min, or 6x10-min
- Cancel anytime via Account -> Manage billing
- USD pricing, all major debit/credit cards, GPay, Apple Pay, local wallets
