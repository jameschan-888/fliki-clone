100+ templates across every category

AI-fillable scenes, not static layouts

Brand kit baked in

Every aspect ratio from one template

Bulk render the same template across rows

How it works

How to make a video with a template in 4 steps

From a one-line topic to a finished branded video in under 5 minutes. The template is the skeleton; Fliki fills the body.

Step 1

Browse and pick a template

Filter by category, format, or aspect ratio. Each template includes a video preview so you know what the finished render looks like before you start.

Step 2

Drop your topic or script

Type a one-line topic, paste a full script, drop a blog URL, or upload a PPT. Fliki splits the input into scenes that match the template structure.

Step 3

Voice, brand, customise

Pick a voice from 2,000+ options, apply the brand kit, swap any clip or AI-generate fresh visuals per scene, layer music, and burn in captions in 100+ languages.

Step 4

Export and publish

Render 1080p MP4 (or 4K on paid plans) in 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9. Publish directly to YouTube, TikTok, Reels, LinkedIn, or share a hosted link with one click.
