How to create an AI avatar video in 4 steps

From a script or photo to a fully lip-synced presenter video in under 5 minutes. No camera, no mic, no studio.

Step 1

Pick or upload your avatar

Choose from 70+ public AI avatars, upload one photo to generate a talking-photo with OmniHuman 1.5, or request a custom brand avatar on a paid plan.

Step 2

Add your script and voice

Paste a script and pick from 2,000+ neural voices in 80+ languages. Or clone your own voice once with a 30-second sample and reuse it across every avatar video.

Step 3

Generate the talking video

Fliki routes the job to OmniHuman 1.5 (talking-photo), Sync-3 (video lip-sync), or its default presenter engine. Generation typically completes in 2 to 3 minutes per scene.

Step 4

Edit, caption, translate, export

Trim, layer B-roll, add captions, translate to other languages, and export watermark-free 1080p MP4. Publish to YouTube, LinkedIn, Reels, or share a hosted link.
