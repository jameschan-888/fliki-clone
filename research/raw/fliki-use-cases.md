# Fliki Use Cases

> Source: https://fliki.ai/use-cases  Fetched: 2026-07-23

AI video for every team. One workspace, every use case.

## Stats
- 12M+ creators & teams
- 100M+ AI videos created
- 80+ languages
- 50,000+ companies worldwide

## Primary use cases
- Marketing campaigns and video ads
- Social-media content (YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, Reels)
- Sales outreach and demos
- Product training and L&D
- Internal communications
- Educational courses
- Ecommerce product videos
- Content localization

## Core audience segments
1. Individual creators - daily publishing on YouTube, TikTok, podcasts, audiobooks (12M+ creators as of June 2025)
2. SMB marketing/sales/content teams - campaigns, demos, outreach video
3. Enterprise marketing, L&D, internal comms, localization - high-volume, global audiences

## Shared workspace primitives
- Brand kit
- Voice library
- Avatar library
- Template library
- Long-form / short-form / square / audio / audiobook from same project
- Re-editable at script level (line changes re-render affected scene)

## Time / cost benchmarks
- AI video ships in ~15 minutes (vs 5-10 days traditional)
- Production cost drops from $2k-$20k to under $50
- Re-editable at script level

## Localization workflow
- Upload 30-sec voice sample + short reference video
- Cloned voice + AI avatar tied to team member
- Same presenter reads every video
- Per-employee consent workflows (Premium / Enterprise)

## Multi-region publishing
- 80+ languages, 100+ dialects
- One click: script -> voice -> lip-sync -> captions
- Single training video ships in 12 markets without re-recording

## Commercial use
- Exports royalty-free on paid plans
- YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Meta, paid ads, demos, sales outreach, internal training, LMS
- Enterprise plans include indemnification

## API / Integrations
- Programmatic bulk video generation
- HubSpot, Salesforce, Outreach, Webflow native integrations
- Zapier connector (Enterprise)
- Use cases: personalized sales video per lead, localized product video per SKU, custom training module per cohort

## Competitive positioning (as of mid-2025)
- Synthesia / HeyGen: AI-avatar-first, lighter on script / b-roll / music / multi-aspect
- Pictory: long-form to clips, narrow use case
- InVideo: template-driven editor, limited AI script / avatar cloning
- Fliki: full composer: AI script + 2000+ voices + voice clone + AI avatars + auto-paired visuals + captions + music + every aspect ratio + direct publishing
