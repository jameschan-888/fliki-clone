Why creators pick Fliki for script-to-video

A full video, not a templated slideshow

Pictory and Visla pair script lines with stock images. Canva and HeyGen ship one avatar reading the script. LTX Studio is great for cinematic storyboarding but stops at the storyboard. Fliki is the only tool that splits the script, pairs visuals (stock + AI b-roll + AI image-to-video), voices it with 2,000+ neural voices, captions it, and exports a finished MP4 in one workflow.

Automatic scene splitting

Fliki reads the script and breaks it into scenes based on sentence rhythm, topic shifts, and pacing. Each scene becomes a clip on the timeline. No manual scene boundary work, no copy-paste-into-rows.

2,000+ neural voices in 80+ languages

Pick the voice that fits the brand, the topic, and the language via our AI voiceover library. Pace, pitch, and pause control per line. Or clone your voice with a 30-second sample and let it narrate every script you ship.

Auto-paired stock + AI b-roll

Each scene is matched with stock footage from a library of millions, AI-generated clips from our AI video generator, or your own brand assets. Brand kit overlays apply automatically. Swap any clip with one click.

AI script writer if you do not have one yet

No script yet? Type a topic into our AI script writer or AI script generator and Fliki drafts a scene-segmented script ready to feed the script-to-video composer. Idea to finished video in one continuous workflow.

Animated captions in 100+ languages

Burn-in captions generated from the script or AI voiceover. Word-by-word reveal, custom font, colour, position, and animation. Critical for short-form sound-off scroll and accessibility compliance.

AI avatar narration

How it works

How to turn a script into video in 4 steps

From a finished script to a publish-ready video in under 5 minutes for short-form, 10 to 15 minutes for long-form.

Step 1

Paste your script

Drop in any script. A 60-second TikTok, a 5-minute explainer, a 15-minute long-form. Plain text or a formatted document. Fliki handles the split.

Step 2

Pick voice, language, and tone

Choose from 2,000+ neural voices in 80+ languages, or clone your own voice. Set the tone (neutral, marketing, explainer, motivational) so the read matches the script intent.

Step 3

Visuals, captions, and music

Fliki auto-pairs each scene with stock footage, AI b-roll, or your brand assets. Burn-in captions render from the script. Royalty-free music or fresh AI music ducks under the voiceover.

Step 4

Review, resize, export

Tweak any scene, swap a clip, edit a line. Resize for the platform you are publishing to, then export 1080p MP4 (or 4K) ready for YouTube, TikTok, Reels, LinkedIn, or web embed.
