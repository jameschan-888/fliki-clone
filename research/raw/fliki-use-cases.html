AI Video Use Cases FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AI video use cases

What teams use AI video for, which use cases AI video does well, how Fliki maps to common workflows, and how AI video compares to traditional video production. Fliki-usage figures are as of June 2025; third-party statistics cite Wyzowl's State of Video Marketing 2024 (91% of businesses use video as a marketing tool) and HubSpot's Marketing Trends 2024 report unless otherwise noted.

AI video is used across every team that ships video content. According to Wyzowl's 2024 State of Video Marketing, 91% of businesses use video as a marketing tool—up from 61% in 2016. The most common use cases: marketing campaigns and video ads, social-media content (YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, Reels), sales outreach and demos, product training and L&D, internal communications, educational courses, ecommerce product videos, and content localization into multiple languages. AI video replaces or augments traditional video production—fastest gains are on high-volume formats (social, training, localization) and personalized formats (sales).

AI video is best for: (1) high-volume formats where a team needs many videos a week (social, ads, sales outreach); (2) localized content that ships in many languages; (3) explainer, training, and tutorial content driven by a script; (4) talking-head video where the same presenter or avatar reads many scripts; (5) faceless creator content (finance, history, listicles). It is worth being direct about the limits: AI video is notably weaker for documentary-style filmed content, live events, and high-budget brand films where authentic on-location cinematography, human performance nuance, and directorial craft carry the primary creative value—those formats still benefit from traditional production crews. Knowing this upfront helps teams put the right tool against the right format.
